⚔ Swordland Battle Guide

Generals

  • Monitor enemy movements.
  • Be first to capture uncontested buildings in Phase 1.
  • Please don't hoard free healing speedups: Swordland healing is free.
  • Please don't hoard free march speedups: Swordland march time reduction is free.

How to Earn Relic Points

Understanding where points come from helps you choose the best strategy.

Source Alliance Points Personal Points Notes
First Capture Large one-time bonus Large one-time bonus The first alliance to capture a building receives a major bonus.
Building Occupation Continuous points Continuous points Sword Shrine and Sanctums provide the highest point income.
Loot Crates Bonus points Bonus points Spawn when a building is captured from the enemy.
Undercellars Burst points Burst points Appear randomly during the End Game. Watch the minimap.
Combat (Attack) None 80 points per 10,000 enemy Power defeated Only grants Personal Points.
Combat (Defense) None 40 points per 10,000 enemy Power defeated Half the points earned from attacking.

Attackers

Primary Objectives

  • Capture the assigned buildings. Assign 1–2 attackers to each building with 2–3 defenders. Use rallies when required.
  • Secure all priority buildings within the first 10 minutes.
  • Maintain control after capture.
  • Focus on Stables, Sanctums, and Bell Tower first.
  • At 12 minutes, capture Sword Shrine and Mercenary Camp.
  • Once priority buildings are secured, begin burning enemy cities.

Responsibilities

  • Garrison captured buildings with your strongest defensive formation.
  • Support nearby rallies whenever possible.
  • If overwhelmed, relocate to another priority building.
  • Remain at your assigned objective unless instructed otherwise.

Defenders

Primary Objectives

  • Know which building you are assigned to defend.
  • Identify your assigned attacker.
  • Support attacker rallies using Chenko and Yeonwoo.

Responsibilities

  • After a building is captured, immediately send your strongest defensive march.
  • Place Saul or Gordon in the left hero slot.
  • Continue reinforcing rallies.
  • Spread your cities to avoid giving enemies easy targets.
  • Reinforce allied cities whenever needed.
  • If a building is lost, immediately gather nearby resources.
  • Gather Undercellars using spare marches.
  • Maintain control of Stables, Sanctums, Sword Shrine, Mercenary Camp, and two Abbeys whenever possible.

Phase 1 — 57:00 to 45:01

Capture Priority

  1. Stables
  2. Furthest Sanctum
  3. Closest Sanctum
  4. Bell Tower
  5. Upper Abbey
  6. Lower Abbey
Important
  • Your first teleport is free—use it to position near your assigned target immediately
  • Empty buildings are wasted opportunities—even weak defenders prevent solo captures
  • Additional Attackers should burn enemy cities to force teleports, disrupting their positioning.
  • Defenders should not remain at the base. Instead, teleport between two or three nearby buildings where enemy players cannot easily spot your city.
  • Don't overcommit to one building; spread defenders across multiple structures
  • Stables and Sanctums remain the highest priority.

Phase 2 ― 45:00

Priority Order
  • Sword Shrine
  • Mercenary Camp
  • Reformation (only if your alliance has a clear advantage)
  • Sanctums
  • Stables

Phase 3 ― Endgame (Final 15 Minutes)

  • Capture Undercellars using spare marches.
  • Maintain control of all occupied buildings.
  • Defenders must immediately request support if under pressure.
  • When appropriate, attackers should continuously burn enemy cities using rallies or solo attacks.
Last Push Strategy (at 06:00) if we are losing on points or becoming overwhelmed
  • Launch a double rally on the Swordshrine, led by our two strongest rally leaders. ratio 50-20-30
  • All available players must be ready to join the rally immediately.
  • If the rally succeeds, defenders who have a Teleport item should immediately teleport around the Swordshrine (center).
  • Secure and collect the Loot Crates before the enemy can reach them.
